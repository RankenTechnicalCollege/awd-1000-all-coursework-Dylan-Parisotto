About Us

Shake It Off was founded in 2020 by a group of dog lovers who wanted to make grooming more accessible and convenient for busy pet owners.

Our team of professional groomers is dedicated to providing high-quality services in a friendly and welcoming environment.

We believe that every dog deserves to look and feel their best, and we are committed to making that happen for our clients.

Dog Grooming Services
Service Recommended Frequency Description
Bath & Brush Every 4–6 Weeks Includes a bath, blow dry, and thorough brushing.
Haircut & Styling Every 6–8 Weeks Breed-specific or customized trims for your dog.
Nail Trimming Every 3–4 Weeks Keeps nails short and comfortable for walking.
Ear Cleaning Monthly Removes wax and debris to help prevent infections.
Teeth Brushing Weekly Helps maintain fresh breath and healthy teeth.
