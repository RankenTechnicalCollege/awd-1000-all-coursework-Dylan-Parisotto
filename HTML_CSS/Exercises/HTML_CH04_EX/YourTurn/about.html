About Us

Shake It Off was founded in 2020 by a group of dog lovers who wanted to make grooming more accessible and convenient for busy pet owners.

Our team of professional groomers is dedicated to providing high-quality services in a friendly and welcoming environment.

We believe that every dog deserves to look and feel their best, and we are committed to making that happen for our clients.
